## About the book

The complete revised book contains twenty chapters, original field photography, session accounts and the conference programme. Chapters can be read individually. Executive summaries and indexes of tables, figures and photographs help readers navigate the material.

## Topics covered

- Leadership, hospitality and people development, including **Inside Panda with Andrew Cherng**.
- Customers, franchising and the operational requirements of growth.
- M&A, private equity, financing and restaurant real estate.
- Delivery, first-party ordering, beverage trends and restaurant turnarounds.
- AI applications in restaurant operations, clearly labelled GII interpretation and the author's personal postscript.

## Reporting approach

Speaker claims are attributed as conference observations. The book separates field reporting and public-document evidence from GII interpretation and illustrative calculations.
